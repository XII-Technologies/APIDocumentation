Detailed Description

Reusable CPU geometry for scene and asset-preview grids. Submit GetLines() through xiiDebugRenderer; the grid owns no GPU resources, passes, world objects, or persistent render data.

Perspective grids span 50 steps either side of the supplied origin/rotation (scale is ignored). Orthographic grids fill the viewport at a depth behind its scene and align with the camera axes. Their spacing increases by powers of ten to retain at least ten pixels between lines and bound work to at most 2050 intervals per axis. Orthographic global grids snap to the world origin; local grids snap to the supplied origin. Zero spacing or a zero transform scale disables the grid successfully. Invalid settings clear the output and return failure. Storage capacity is retained across builds.
