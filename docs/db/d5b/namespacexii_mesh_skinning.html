Detailed Description

Copyright (c) Theophilus Eriata. All Rights Reserved.

Shared CPU skinning operations for spatial bounds and consumers of native mesh geometry. Palettes contain mesh-space skinning matrices, including the inverse bind transform. These operations allocate no storage and leave their output unchanged on failure.

xiiResult xiiMeshSkinning::ComputeBounds ( const xiiBoundingBoxSphere & bindBounds,
xiiArrayPtr< const xiiMat4 > palette,
xiiBoundingBoxSphere & out_bounds )
nodiscard

Conservatively encloses every normalized positive blend of points in bindBounds. Includes bind pose for vertices without influences. Uses the union of transformed boxes, so shear and singular affine transforms remain conservative. No CPU vertex retention or GPU readback is required. O(palette size); potentially loose for large rigs.

xiiVec3 & out_position )
nodiscard

Evaluates a position with native ushort4 indices and normalized byte4 weights. Nonzero weights are renormalized by their sum; an empty palette or all-zero weights preserves the bind position. Only nonzero influences require valid palette indices. Referenced matrices and the resulting position must be finite and affine. O(1).
